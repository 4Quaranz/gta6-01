SUNPORT

Sunport is a sunny coastal city that never sleeps — and never forgets a debt. You are Jace Moreno, a getaway driver pulled into one last crew with Lena Cruz. Steal cars, dodge the Sunport PD, and run four jobs in Sunport Nights — from a boosted ride to the Big One at Sunport First Bank.

WASD move / drive  ·  Mouse look  ·  Shift run
E enter / exit car  ·  F punch  ·  Click shoot
R reload  ·  Space jump / handbrake  ·  M mute
P pause  ·  H help. Stealing cars and hurting people raises your wanted level — hide to cool off.
Touch: left stick to move / drive, drag right side to look, buttons to enter, punch, shoot and reload.
  • Follow the yellow beacon and the minimap marker to each job.
  • Getting busted or wasted restarts the current mission — and costs you at the hospital.
